Day One · Trek & Beach

Mountains & Shore — What to Wear

Four outfits. One incredible day. Zero wardrobe stress.

I
Trekking Outfit + Shoes
Comfortable clothes for Kodachadri — night dress is perfectly fine too. Bring shoes with good grip for the trail.
II
Beach Dress
A separate outfit if you plan to play in the water — something you don't mind getting wet and sandy.
III
Photoshoot Outfit
One fresh, good-looking pair for photos and visiting remaining spots after your swim.
IV
Night Dress
Whatever helps you sleep like a log after an action-packed Day 1.

Day Two · Temple & Beach

Sacred Mornings, Sandy Evenings

Respect the temple dress code. Then hit the beach. Four outfits cover everything.

I
Traditional Dress — Temple Visit
Mandatory for entry. Read the gender-specific dress code and the prohibited list carefully below.
For Boys
Dhoti or full-length trousers/pants. Keep it traditional and dignified.
For Girls
Saree, half-saree, or salwar kameez with dupatta. Traditional, modest Indian attire only.
⛔ Strictly Prohibited — Both Men & Women

Shorts · Half-pants · Three-fourths · Jeans · Skirts · Leggings · Ripped jeans · Sleeveless or revealing outfits.
No exceptions. Temple authorities enforce this at the entrance.

II
Casual Change After Temple
One relaxed pair to change into after the temple visit — completely optional.
III
Extra Beach Outfit
Planning to play in the water on Day 2 as well? Bring one extra pair for the beach.
IV
Night Dress
Comfortable sleepwear to wind down after an equally epic Day 2.

Carry These · Both Days

The Essentials Checklist

Don't leave home without these. Your future self will thank you.

🪥
Toiletries
Toothpaste, brush, soap & any personal items you need daily.
🍿
Load Up on Snacks
Sweet, spicy, savory — bring an abundance. Best snacks = best vibes.
💊
Medical Kit
Headache, cold, fever tablets & your personal medications.
🧴
Sunscreen & Skincare
Girls: sunscreen, body lotion & makeup essentials. Boys: sunscreen is a must!
🔋
Powerbank & Speaker
If you own one, bring it — will be a lifesaver for everyone on the trip.
🏐
Sports Ball
Volleyball or any ball — beach evening game session awaits!
💧
Water Bottle
Stay hydrated always — especially critical on the Kodachadri trek.
🧺
Towel + personal essentials
Don't forget a towel and an extra set of personal essentials — small things, big comfort.
🕶️
Sunglasses & Cap
The sun hits differently at the beach and on open mountain trails.
🎒
Small Backpack
For the trek — carry water, snacks & essentials hands-free.
